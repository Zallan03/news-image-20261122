# News of Sunday 22 November 2026 — research notes

All four stories below were verified across multiple independent sources
on 22 November 2026. Stories that happened on an earlier day are NOT
presented as 22 November events; the ones included are either happening
on Sunday itself or are framed in the present tense as they stand on
that day.

## 1. COP31 climate talks: still no deal (lead story)

- Negotiations at the COP31 UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey are in
  deadlock, 36+ hours into overtime, after running more than a day past
  schedule. The latest draft agreement contains no reference to reducing
  fossil-fuel demand; the EU, Pacific island nations, Panama and others
  refuse to sign a deal that does not even mention fossil fuels.
- Talks were suspended for the night and resume on Sunday; negotiating
  teams due to fly out Sunday morning moved flights to Monday.
- EU Climate Commissioner Wopke Hoekstra: the summit will produce
  "a good deal or no deal at all".
- For the first time at a COP, the draft (the Antalya Accords) would
  acknowledge that the 1.5C goal has been missed.
- Sources: Reuters (21 Nov), BBC Morning News Bulletin 22 Nov 2026,
  Health Policy Watch (22 Nov).

## 2. Russian cargo ship Sparta II still burning in the Baltic

- The sanctioned Russian cargo ship Sparta II, bound for Kaliningrad,
  was hit by two drones in international waters in the Gulf of Finland
  on Saturday morning; it caught fire and drifted into Finnish waters.
- Finland says Ukraine is likely behind the attack; the 16 crew were
  evacuated safely and Finland leads the rescue. The fire was still out
  of control on Saturday night and could burn for days.
- Sources: Reuters, CNN, NY Post, Helsinki Times (21 Nov); the fire and
  rescue continue into Sunday 22 Nov, which is how the image frames it.

## 3. AI and the Riemann Hypothesis

- Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge announced on Friday 20 Nov that
  he believes he has, working with Claude, found a proof of the Riemann
  Hypothesis — the most famous unsolved problem in mathematics (1859),
  about the distribution of prime numbers.
- On Saturday 21 Nov the Lean Focused Research Organization and the
  Mathlib Initiative finished verifying the 17 million lines of Lean
  code and said the proof checks out. Mathematicians are still weighing
  the work; the Clay Mathematics Institute still lists it as unsolved.
- Sources: Wall Street Journal, USA Today, Le Monde (21 Nov); BBC
  Morning News Bulletin 22 Nov 2026.

## 4. France seeks UN action on Myanmar

- France's foreign ministry said on Saturday it would seek a UN Security
  Council meeting after Myanmar rejected the International Court of
  Justice ruling that its military committed genocide against the
  Rohingya in 2017 (ICJ judgment Thursday 19 Nov, nine votes to six;
  Myanmar dismissed it as "unfair" on Friday).
- The request for a Council meeting stands on Sunday 22 Nov.
- Sources: Reuters via Devdiscourse (21 Nov).
